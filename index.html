What areas does 804Michael serve?

804Michael, Michael Hottman, a Broker with Malcolm Real Estate, serves Ashland, Hanover County, Mechanicsville, Glen Allen, Short Pump, Henrico County, and the broader Richmond metropolitan area in Central Virginia, including Chesterfield, Powhatan, Goochland, and Caroline counties.

Why is Ashland, VA called the "Center of the Universe"?

Ashland earned the moniker "Center of the Universe" due to its central geographic location within Hanover County and the state, combined with proud, small-town civic pride. The phrase was popularized by former town mayor Richard "Dick" Gillis during his tenure from 1977 to 1990 and has since become the town's official, celebrated identity.

Do I need a buyer-broker agreement in Virginia?

Yes, Virginia law (Virginia Code § 54.1-2132) requires real estate agents to have a signed written buyer-broker (representation) agreement with a prospective buyer before showing them any property. This agreement clearly defines the brokerage relationship, services provided, agreement duration, and compensation terms. Download a Sample Buyer Broker Agreement here.

What disclosures are required when selling a home in Virginia?

Very few disclosures are required from sellers in Virginia so buyers need to exercise extreme due diligence when buying homes. As of 2026 there are 19 specific items the state has identified that sellers DO NOT need to disclose and 10 very specific disclosures that apply for limited properties. This list changes on an annual basis so you need a good agent to help navigate these pitfalls. For more information see the Virginia DPOR disclosure guidance.

How do I choose the best real estate agent in Central Virginia?

Look beyond high transaction volume or low commission fees. Instead, choose a local agent whose communication style and values genuinely fit yours. You need someone who actually lives in and knows the area, not an oversized team or "big agent" that does lots of transactions. A great local agent brings a perspective and care to protect your transaction and prevent costly contract pitfalls.
