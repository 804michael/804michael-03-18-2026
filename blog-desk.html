Source feeds

YouTube channels use https://www.youtube.com/feeds/videos.xml?channel_id=UC… — open the channel, View Source, and search for channelId to get the UC id. Most WordPress sites work by adding /feed/ to the address. A subreddit works as https://www.reddit.com/r/NAME/top/.rss?t=week. A feed that answers but has no items shows as failed after the next scan, which is how a dead feed gets caught.

Refresh radar

Published posts past six months. After the first year, updating one of these usually beats writing something new: the URL already has history with Google, so a refreshed post recovers rankings far faster than a new one earns them.
