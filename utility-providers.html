Utility Providers for Central Virginia

A quick-reference guide for sellers and buyers in Ashland, Hanover County & the greater Richmond area

› Dev › Utility Providers

Know Who to Call

Coverage varies address by address in Central Virginia — a home a few miles apart from another can be on a different electric co-op, a different water system, or no natural gas at all. Use these as a starting point, then confirm exact availability for the specific address before closing or moving in.

Utilities

Electric

Most of Ashland and inner Hanover County is served by Dominion Energy Virginia; rural western and northern Hanover — along with several surrounding counties — falls in Rappahannock Electric Cooperative (REC) territory. Check both if you're unsure which serves a specific address.

Dominion Energy Virginia Start, stop or transfer electric service online. Rappahannock Electric Cooperative (REC) Member-owned electric co-op serving rural Hanover and neighboring counties — new service sign-up.
Utilities

Water & Sewer

Public water and sewer in Hanover County is limited to certain service areas — much of the county, especially rural parcels, relies on a private well and septic system instead. Confirm which applies to a specific property; see the Due Diligence page for well/septic questions to ask.

Hanover County Public Utilities Residential water & sewer customer info. New service: call Customer Service at 804-365-6024.
Utilities

Natural Gas & Heating Fuel

Natural gas service is limited outside town centers — many rural Hanover homes heat with propane, oil, or electric heat pumps instead. Check gas availability for a specific address before assuming it's an option.

Columbia Gas of Virginia Check natural-gas availability for an address, or start/transfer gas service.

If natural gas isn't available, ask a seller (or check inspection records) for the age and fuel type of the furnace/heat pump and, where applicable, the propane tank ownership (owned vs. leased) — that affects who you'd call to fill it.

Utilities

Internet & Broadband

Cable and fiber are common in and around Ashland and Mechanicsville; more rural pockets of Hanover have historically had fewer options, though that's changing through the county's "Connect Hanover" fiber expansion.

Xfinity (Comcast) Cable internet — widely available in and around Ashland, Mechanicsville & Glen Allen. Verizon Fios Fiber internet where available — coverage is pocketed, so check the specific address. All Points Broadband Fiber provider for Hanover's "Connect Hanover" broadband-expansion project, aimed at previously unserved/underserved areas of the county.
Utilities

Trash & Recycling

Hanover County doesn't run county-wide curbside trash collection. Most residents in unincorporated areas either contract with a private hauler for curbside pickup or use one of the county's residential-only trash centers.

Common private haulers in the area include Republic Services, WM (Waste Management), and GFL Environmental — availability and pricing vary by neighborhood, so ask a specific provider whether they already serve the street, or check with the HOA if the home is in one (some HOAs contract service for the whole neighborhood).
